How It Works

Three simple steps to get started with your attachment or internship

1

Select Department

Choose the department you were assigned to by Parliament's HR office.

2

Register or Login

Create your account as a new student or log in if you are already registered.

3

Access Dashboard

Submit daily task logs, track your attachment period, and receive supervisor feedback.

4

Complete Attachment

Your access closes automatically after your 3-month period or upon supervisor sign-off.
